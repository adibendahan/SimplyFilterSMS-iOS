Simply Filter SMS 4.0.0 is here! 🎉

• A Fresh Look - A refreshed app icon, redesigned screens, and smoother animations throughout.
• Community Lists - Subscribe to filter lists shared by other people, or share your own. Lists update automatically when their authors publish a new version. Find them under Your lists on the home screen.
• Siri & Shortcuts - Add, remove and check filters with Siri, or from the Shortcuts app.
• Search & Spotlight - Find any filter, list, setting or answer from Search on the home screen, or from iPhone Spotlight.

Plus bug fixes and improvements.
