# Communication Rules

- **One question per reply.** When several topics come up, say how many there are, then raise them one at a time, highest priority first, waiting for an answer before the next. This covers review findings: one issue, with its exact before and after, then wait.
- **Short answers to simple questions.** A sentence or two; no headers, option lists or proposals unless asked.
- **Explain in plain words,** as if looking at the phone, not at a framework. No acronyms or framework trivia that weren't asked for.
- **When asked what's running,** answer with the literal command and its literal output.
